# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Argue about code, not about each other.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on the work, not the person. "This query runs on every request" is
  useful; "this is amateur code" is not.
- Assume the other person knows something you do not. Most disagreements in a codebase
  turn out to be missing context rather than bad judgement.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time and may decline a change. A "no" to a pull
  request is not a verdict on you.
- Respect that contributors are volunteers with other commitments, including the
  maintainers.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments — including about someone's
  background, identity, or level of experience.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing issues and pull
  requests.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any other
project space, and to conduct elsewhere that affects someone's safety in this project.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's report form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) or by opening a
[security advisory](https://github.com/PhantomPixelDev/modulo-cms/security/advisories/new),
which is private and works for conduct reports too.

Reports are handled confidentially. Depending on severity, the response may be a
warning, removal of comments, or a ban from the project. Maintainers who violate this
document are subject to the same consequences as anyone else.
